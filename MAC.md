MAC = Media Access Control Address

- colon or hyphen separated set of hexadecimal numbers
- Example: fa:34:f3:a5:09:ba
- MAC addresses are of 6 bytes or 6 \* 8 = 48 bits
- OUI = Organizationally Unique Identifier
- First 3 bytes or 24 bits are reserved for OUI
